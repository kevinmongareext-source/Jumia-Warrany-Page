Your warranty questions, answered

Jumia works with trusted suppliers and vendors to bring you the best and most affordable products. If something goes wrong that isn't your fault, our brands back that commitment with a warranty — so your product can be repaired or replaced. Warranty coverage is available on selected brands and products.

What is covered?

  • Manufacturing defects
  • Mechanical or electrical faults covered by the manufacturer's warranty
  • Issues occurring within the stated warranty period
Coverage varies by product and manufacturer. Please refer to the specific warranty terms for your item.

Usually not covered

  • Accidental damage
  • Misuse or improper handling
  • Normal wear and tear
  • Damage caused by unauthorized repairs

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about your warranty.

How do I know if my product has a warranty?

On the product page, there's a dedicated warranty field to the right of the product. If the product carries a warranty, this field shows the duration.

You can also check at the top of the page, just above the product name, where a warranty badge appears if the product is covered.

Example: the warranty badge appears above the product name, next to the Official Store tag.

At checkout, you can also see it listed alongside your delivery options — the Warranty field there shows the coverage period for that product.

Example: warranty duration shown alongside delivery and return options at checkout.
How can I activate my warranty?

Within 7 days of delivery: call Jumia — your product may still be returnable without using the warranty. Check our Return Policy for details.

Over 7 days after delivery, with a warranty: contact the service center listed on our warranty page, or call Jumia and we'll provide the contact for you.

Over 7 days after delivery, with no warranty: unfortunately Jumia is unable to cover the item, and you'll be responsible for the repair cost.

How do I go about servicing my product?

Send the product directly to the service center stated on your warranty card, if you have one, or the one given to you by our customer service team. For a prompt warranty claim, please include:

  • All original accessories and packaging
  • Proof of purchase from Jumia (your invoice)
Where are the service centers?

Please refer to the manufacturer or service center details on the warranty card included with your product. If there's no warranty card, check the user manual or product packaging for more details. You can also refer to our service center directory.

If you still can't find your service center, reach out to us on our CS number 0711 011 011 or 0730 868 000 and we'll be happy to assist you.

If my product is within warranty duration, do I need to pay for repairs?

If your product is within warranty duration and is damaged by a mechanical or electrical fault, you don't have to worry about the repair cost.

You're covered — no repair cost
If my product is out of warranty duration, do I need to pay for repairs?

If your product is out of warranty duration, you'll be responsible for the repair cost. We suggest repairing the product at a service center authorized by the manufacturer for better quality service.

Repair cost applies
